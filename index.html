Dotted guides show terrain landmarks, not your predicted route.

Sparse dotted marks indicate terrain heights, edges and the maximum horizontal energy reach. They never reveal your plotted line, endpoint or predicted outcome. Work out the equation first. A distant line makes the Graphling shrug without spending a specialist charge.

Physical workers

A Rampwright builds a walkable bridge ahead and walks on it. A Trencher digs through earth and leaves a usable tunnel. Both start with 10 energy, stop at obstructions or when energy runs out, then return to ordinary walking.
